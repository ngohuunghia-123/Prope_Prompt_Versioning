# A-153 — Controlled labeling may read redacted retained bodies

A workspace may opt in to manual labeling of production calls. Only the labeling permission can read a retained call body, redaction occurs before the response leaves the server, and every access is audited. Labels are human evidence for PropAI suggestions; they never authorize an automatic revision or release.

This supersedes A-105 and ADR-0018 only for the narrow labeling read path. Retention, tenant isolation and the prohibition on bodies in logs, metrics and property-history details remain unchanged.
