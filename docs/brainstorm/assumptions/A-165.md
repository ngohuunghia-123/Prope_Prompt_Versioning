# A-165 - A window comparison is enough to call degradation, and a baseline model is not built

**Statement**: every ledger detector in `prompt-improvement` compares one 24-hour window with the one
immediately before it. Nothing learns a prompt's normal shape over time, and nothing is seasonal-aware.

**Rationale**: the alternative is a baseline model, which is a second thing to be wrong about and one
nobody can read. A finding somebody can check — *fallback went from 1% of calls to 22%, here are both
counts* — is arguable in a way that *the model says this is anomalous* is not, and this module's whole
posture is that the arithmetic half must be defensible without trust.

**What it costs, stated plainly**: a prompt whose traffic is seasonal can produce a finding that is a
calendar artefact. A month-end batch job, a Monday spike, a marketing send — each of these can move a
share past both thresholds honestly and mean nothing. `MIN_REQUESTS` and the paired
relative-plus-absolute test in `detectors._rose` cut the smallest of these out; they do not cut out a
genuine weekly rhythm.

**What makes it survivable**: nothing blocks (ADR-0068, inheriting ADR-0067 D-03), every such finding
is `advice` or a dismissible `warning`, and the dismissal reason is recorded per `detector_id`. If the
reasons cluster on seasonality, that IS the evidence that justifies building the baseline — which is a
better position to build one from than a guess made before any traffic existed.
