# A-166 - The estimated weekly probe cost is arithmetic on the price row, not a measurement of past spend

**Statement**: the figure shown at the moment somebody enables the re-probe schedule is computed from
the price row in force and the shipped catalogue's case count. It is not computed from the last four
weeks of measured probe spend.

**Rationale**: the two disagree the week a price changes, and one of them has to be picked. The price
row wins for a reason that is about the moment rather than the accuracy: the figure is shown at
ENABLEMENT, when there may be no probe history at all — and a measurement that does not exist renders
as zero, which reads as free. A number that is wrong by a price revision is a smaller failure than a
number that says a recurring charge costs nothing.

**What it costs**: the estimate lags a price change by however long it takes somebody to notice, and it
cannot account for a workspace whose prompts are unusually long. It is labelled an estimate, which the
arithmetic honestly is.

**What the alternative would need**: a measured figure would have to fall back to the price row anyway
for a first-time enabler, so it is the price-row calculation plus a second code path — and the second
path is the one that would be exercised least and tested least.
