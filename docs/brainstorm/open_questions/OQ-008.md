# OQ-008 — Is an owner one person or a team, and what happens on transfer or departure?

## Question
Can a prompt property have exactly one owning person, or can a team own it? Can there be stewards
alongside the owner? How is a transfer recorded, and what happens to a prompt whose owner leaves the
organisation?

## Why it matters
This is the shape of the ownership field and of the accountability story the product is named after.
`BRIEF.md` §7 makes "owner" one of the three fields the day-90 test checks; an orphaned owner is
exactly the reconciliation-queue failure `positioning.md` §11 warns about, drawing on the CMDB
precedent.

## What we know already
- `prope_source_of_truth.md` §20 asks all four sub-questions and answers none.
- `BRIEF.md` §13 question 5 repeats them with a Week-1 deadline.
- The language system already fixes the action name: **Transfer ownership**, with confirmation.

## Options
1. One person; transfer only; orphaned prompts land in a reconciliation queue.
2. One person plus named stewards who may edit but not transfer.
3. A team owns; individuals are members.

## Recommendation
Option 1 for the MVP — it is the only one that keeps the accountability claim literally true, and
the reconciliation queue is already the prescribed design response. Confirm or overrule.
