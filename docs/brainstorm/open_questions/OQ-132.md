# OQ-132 — revision 0006 amends three seeded `detail_keys` sets that 0005 shipped empty

**Raised by the access-control build, 2026-08-11.** `blocks_build: false` — the amendment shipped,
it is argued in revision 0006's docstring, and `tests/integration/test_history_schema_ships_its_guarantees.py`
asserts the resulting five-entry map.

## The deviation

| Action | Revision 0005 | Revision 0006 |
|---|---|---|
| `access_granted` | `{}` | `{grantee_user_id, level}` |
| `access_revoked` | `{}` | `{grantee_user_id, level}` |
| `role_revoked` | `{}` | `{role, grantee_user_id}` |

`role_assigned` already carried `{role, grantee_user_id}` at 0005 and is untouched.

## Why

**AC-12 names the payload and the registry had no slot for it.** The acceptance criterion requires a
grant event to carry *"actor, grantee, level, and timestamp"*. Three of those four are envelope
columns on `history_event`; `grantee_user_id` and `level` are not, and `append()` refuses an
unregistered key with `unknown_detail_key`. So the criterion was unmeetable as seeded.

**Reading the level off the `prompt_grant` row instead does not work.** A revocation *deletes* that
row, so the revocation event would point at nothing and *"how many editor grants are live"* would
become unanswerable from the trail the moment anything was revoked.

**`role_revoked` was asymmetric with `role_assigned`.** An asymmetric pair is worse than either
symmetric one, because a reader believes the two are comparable: the trail said a role had been taken
away and could not say which one, from whom.

The 0005 payloads were derived from a lock-file `outputs` block that lists event *names* and is silent
on detail keys, so the empty sets were an absence of information rather than a decision.

## What is being asked

1. **Confirm the amendment**, in which case `docs/modules/access-control.lock.yml`'s `outputs` block
   should gain the detail keys upstream so the spec and the registry agree.
2. **Or reverse it**, which means AC-12 cannot be met and should be amended instead.

Reversal cost: low today (a `detail_keys` update and three `events.py` builders), rising once a pilot
has written grant events whose payloads a reversal would orphan.

## What is affected either way

- `alembic/versions/0006_access_schema_and_bootstrap.py` — `AMEND_ACTION_REGISTRY`, and `downgrade()`
- `src/prope/access_control/events.py` — `access_granted`, `access_revoked`, `role_revoked`
- `tests/integration/test_history_schema_ships_its_guarantees.py` — `DECLARED_DETAIL_KEYS`
- `docs/modules/*.lock.yml` — **not edited**
