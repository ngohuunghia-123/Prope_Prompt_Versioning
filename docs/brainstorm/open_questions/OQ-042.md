# OQ-042 — A-115 and INT-001 give two different names to two of prompt-registry's permissions, and one action has no declared caller

**Raised by Phase 7 planning, 2026-08-11.** `blocks_build: false` — Phase 7 seeds A-115's names and
records the deviation here. Raised because the seed is what `GET /api/v1/permissions` renders, and the
whole point of `role_permission` being rows (ADR-0020) is that OQ-006's signed-off matrix can be
compared against the running one. A comparison against a list with two names in dispute is a
comparison nobody can complete.

## The disagreement, line by line

`docs/brainstorm/assumptions/A-115.md` names, among its twenty-five:

> `prompt.transfer_owner`, `prompt.grant`, `revision.create`

`docs/architecture/interfaces.md` INT-001, **The action vocabulary is closed**, prompt-registry's row:

> `prompt.read`, `prompt.edit`, `prompt.create`, `prompt.retire`, `prompt.change_owner`,
> `prompt.change_access`

Three findings:

1. **`prompt.transfer_owner` (A-115) and `prompt.change_owner` (INT-001) are the same authority.**
   `docs/modules/prompt-registry.lock.yml` calls the event `owner_changed`, which supports neither name
   over the other.
2. **`prompt.grant` (A-115) and `prompt.change_access` (INT-001) are the same authority — probably.**
   They may also be two: `access_level_changed` is prompt-registry's own event on a field it owns,
   while `access_granted` / `access_revoked` are access-control's events on `prompt_grant`
   (`docs/modules/access-control.lock.yml` `outputs`, corrected 2026-08-08). If the field and the grant
   are separate authorities then **both names are needed and neither list is complete**, which is the
   reading Phase 7 finds most likely and cannot confirm.
3. **`revision.create` appears in A-115 and in no caller's row of INT-001's table.** A-112 requires it —
   *"Editor may additionally create revisions"* — so it is a real permission with a real grantee and no
   registered consumer.

## What Phase 7 does

Seeds and CHECK-constrains `access.role_permission.action` to **A-115's twenty-five names verbatim**,
on the single ground that `docs/modules/access-control.lock.yml` `data.owns` names A-115 and not
INT-001 as the constraint's source: *"`action` is constrained to the closed vocabulary (A-115)"*. No
file under `docs/` is edited (D-99). Phase 13, which builds prompt-registry, is where the wrong choice
surfaces as a `may()` call that always denies.

## What is being asked

Which pair of names is canonical, and whether `prompt.grant` and `prompt.change_access` are one action
or two. If two, A-115 grows by one and INT-001's prompt-registry row grows by one.

## What is affected either way

- The `access.role_permission` seed and its CHECK constraint (one migration, re-seedable)
- `src/prope/access_control/vocabulary.py`
- Phase 13's prompt-registry endpoints, which pass one of these strings to `may()` on every request
- `GET /api/v1/permissions`, whose output is the artifact OQ-006's answer is checked against
