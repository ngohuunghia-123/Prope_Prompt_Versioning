# OQ-006 — Will the pilot hold real corporate prompts, and what is the RBAC / data baseline?

## Question
Do the prompts entered during the pilot and the demo include real internal prompts (possibly
containing business logic, customer-data references, or credentials), or only synthetic and
volunteered non-sensitive content? And what is the approved role matrix, data classification,
retention period, and handling rule for sensitive content?

## Why it matters
`business-analysis.md` §11 rates "no permission model, data classification, retention, or sensitive
content policy" as **Critical**, and both it and `positioning.md` §12 state the rule as
non-negotiable: settle the RBAC matrix and security baseline **before importing real prompts**.
Security and data concerns are the #2 adoption barrier in the survey. Retention duration and
regulatory status are decisions I am not permitted to make.

## What we know already
- The constraint "edit / review / release are separate permissions" is fixed (NFR-01), but the
  concrete matrix is missing.
- A-007 records my working role set (Owner, Reviewer, Viewer, Admin) as a placeholder only.
- `business-analysis.md` §13 question 8 warns against assuming the EU AI Act applies; the applicable
  Vietnamese regime is unconfirmed, and `positioning.md` §7.1 says internal platforms are likely out
  of scope of Nghị định 142/2026/NĐ-CP.

## Options
1. Synthetic and volunteered prompts only for the whole build; defer the baseline to post-pilot.
2. Real prompts, with the RBAC matrix, classification, and retention signed off first.

## Recommendation
Option 1 for the build, option 2 before any real pilot. But retention duration and regulatory status
are yours, and A-020 is only a holding position until you answer.

**Amended 2026-08-08.** The one part of this worth answering *before* the build rather than before the
pilot is the identity provider, because it is the only half that changes what gets written rather than
