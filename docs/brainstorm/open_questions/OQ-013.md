# OQ-013 — Does the gateway store request and response bodies, or only usage metadata?

## Resolution of the first half — 2026-08-08 (user)

> "Prope còn lưu lại lịch sử request/response, sử dụng cho mục đích truy xuất, cải tiến sau này
> (tích hợp LangFuse). Logs sẽ được lưu trong Prope."

Option 3 selected: bodies are stored, in Prope, for traceability and later improvement.

**Consequences applied**
- `00_context.md#scope` — call records now include request and response bodies.
- `positioning.md` §4 "USP hỗ trợ #2" — the content-free-telemetry claim is dead and is recorded as
  a reversal in `00_context.md`. It must be cut from the pitch, not softened.
- The production-input review screen from `positioning.md` §4 — pick the 3–5 real inputs where old
  and new outputs diverge most — becomes reachable in a later release. It is not in this MVP
  (A-004 keeps the suite hand-authored), but the data will exist.
- A-039 bounds the scope: raw call records, not traces or spans, and no observability UI.
- A-040 records a placeholder retention period.

## What is still open — and it is the Critical half

`business-analysis.md` §11 rates the missing sensitive-content policy as **Critical**, and NFR-03
requires retention, encryption, residency, and PII policy to be settled before the pilot. Storing
bodies makes all four urgent rather than theoretical, because the bodies now contain whatever real
end users typed into a consuming product.

1. **Retention.** How long are bodies kept? Metadata is cheap and can be kept indefinitely; bodies
   are the liability. A-040 assumes 30 days as a placeholder.
2. **Who can read a body?** The prompt's owner only, any workspace member, or a separate permission?
   A body may contain another team's customer data, which the prompt's owner has no right to read.
3. **PII and redaction.** Is there a redaction pass before storage, an opt-out per prompt, or
   nothing? And what happens on a deletion request for a specific end user's data?
4. **Residency.** Does any of this have to stay inside Vietnam or inside a specific environment?

## Why I cannot decide these
Retention duration, regulatory status, and sensitive-data handling are on the always-ask list. They
are also the exact items `positioning.md` §12 names as a precondition that the positioning "cannot
solve for itself".

## Recommendation
Answer 1 and 3 before the first real call passes through the gateway. A-020 (no real corporate
prompts until the baseline is signed off) is now doing much heavier lifting than when it was
written, because real traffic means real content, not just real prompt text.
