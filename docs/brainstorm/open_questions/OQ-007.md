# OQ-007 — Is individual mode in this MVP, or is the build enterprise-only?

## Resolution — 2026-08-09 (user)

**Option 1. Out of the MVP; individual mode comes after it.**

The build is the enterprise flow only. Hypothesis H-07 goes untested this cycle and the individual
survey (n=15) informs nothing that ships — accepted knowingly, not by omission. `prope_source_of_truth.md`
§4.4 forbids exposing the governance model as a personal default, so individual mode was never a filter
over these screens; it is a second surface, and it is scheduled after the MVP rather than dropped.

The sweep flagged this as a question the pack had already answered by construction: eight modules,
every one of them built around workspaces, RLS-scoped tenancy and role bindings. Confirming it costs
nothing today and removes a scope risk `BRIEF.md` §12 rated second-largest.

**Consequences applied**: [[A-017]] flips from an agent default to a user decision, and its
`reversibility: moderate` is now the honest label of a post-MVP feature rather than a live risk.


## Question
`prope_source_of_truth.md` §4.4 and Insight 7 describe a separate individual experience —
save → find → reuse → preserve — with different language and no governance surface. Your
five-feature list is entirely the enterprise flow. Is individual mode in this build?

## Why it matters
It is a second product surface and a second copy register, and hypothesis H-07 has its own success
metrics (60% activation, 3 prompts saved in week one, 30% week-two retention). Building it is a
module; not building it means H-07 goes untested and the individual survey (n=15) informs nothing
that ships.

## What we know already
- The individual survey's most-wanted capabilities are one place to keep prompts (8/15) and fast
  search (7/15) — both of which the registry already delivers.
- `prope_source_of_truth.md` §4.4 forbids exposing the full governance model as the personal
  default, so this is not free reuse of the same screens.

## Options
1. Out of MVP. Enterprise flow only.
2. In, as a reduced view over the same registry (review, release, and access controls hidden).
3. In, as a separate surface with its own language.

## Recommendation
Option 1 unless OQ-005 reveals unusual slack. A-017 records that as the working default.
