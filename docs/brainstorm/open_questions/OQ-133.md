# OQ-133 — two documents spell two of the twenty-five permissions differently

**Raised by the access-control build, 2026-08-11.** `blocks_build: false` — A-115's spelling shipped
and the reason is recorded at `src/prope/access_control/actions.py`.

## The disagreement

| A-115 | INT-001's table |
|---|---|
| `prompt.transfer_owner` | `prompt.change_owner` |
| `prompt.grant` | `prompt.change_access` |

Both documents are read-only upstream artefacts and neither is marked superseded.

## What shipped, and why that side

**A-115.** It is what `access.role_permission`'s seed and its `CHECK` constraint are written from, and
it is what `GET /api/v1/permissions` renders. That endpoint exists so OQ-006's eventual answer can be
diffed against the running matrix; rendering a spelling that appears in neither the seed nor the
signed-off matrix would defeat it.

The second pair is also not merely a rename. `prompt.change_access` reads like a permission over the
prompt's `access_level` field, which prompt-registry owns and records as `access_level_changed`;
`prompt.grant` is a permission over `access.prompt_grant`, which access-control owns. If the two
documents mean two different permissions rather than one under two names, the vocabulary is
twenty-six and not twenty-five.

## What is being asked

Pick one spelling per pair, and say whether `prompt.change_access` and `prompt.grant` are the same
permission. Then amend `docs/architecture/interfaces.md` or `A-115` accordingly.

Reversal cost: low while the matrix is a seed. It becomes a migration plus a coordinated rename across
`Action`, the `CHECK`, `ROUTE_ACTION` and every future caller once a workspace's bindings are real.
