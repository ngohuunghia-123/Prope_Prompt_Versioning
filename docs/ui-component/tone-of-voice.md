# Visual tone of voice

## Brand truth first

**Prompt as property.**

**Your prompt. Your property.**

Every image must make this belief felt: a prompt is valuable work with an owner, a history, protected access, and a trusted path into use. Visuals should honour the human judgment behind a prompt—not merely show “AI”, files, or productivity.

The reference landscape is the rendering cue for this belief: luminous, calm, carefully tended, and oriented around a visible path. It is not the brand idea itself, nor a requirement to show nature in every image.

## Name: Quiet stewardship

Quiet stewardship is Prope’s expression of the value in prompt property. It is clear, calm, human, and quietly optimistic: the feeling of valuable work being recognised, cared for, and carried forward with trust.

This is not an AI-fantasy aesthetic. It is a premium editorial illustration language for ownership, provenance, continuity, and trusted use.

## Emotional contract

The image should make people feel:

- “The work behind my prompt has value.”
- “My prompt has an owner, a history, and a place.”
- “There is a clear, safe path from personal craft to trusted use.”
- “This system protects care and judgment without becoming heavy.”

It must not feel bureaucratic, surveillance-driven, crypto-like, playful, childish, threatening, or like a generic stock-technology campaign.

## Core visual grammar

### Light

- Use broad, diffused daylight: early morning, late afternoon, or an overcast-bright interior equivalent.
- The light has a warm, soft golden character but never becomes orange, neon, or cinematic-drama heavy.
- Shadows are long, soft-edged, and legible. Keep highlights creamy rather than pure white.
- Use atmospheric perspective: the foreground is tactile, the middle distance carries the story, and the far distance fades gently.

### Color

- Start from Prope’s warm canvas, deep blue-teal, muted teal, olive green, and restrained yellow accent language.
- Let cool blue/teal create depth and trust; use soft plant/earth greens for care and continuity; reserve yellow-gold for the one meaningful signal, path, marker, or moment of progress.
- Favor low-to-moderate contrast with one calm focal contrast. Avoid broad black, hard pure-white fields, rainbow gradients, saturated purple/blue AI glows, and high-contrast cyberpunk palettes.
- Images must harmonize with `tokens.md`; they should support the UI, never compete with the primary-action yellow.

### Rendering

- Painterly digital illustration with visible but refined brush softness; lightly textured, never rough or sketchy.
- Natural materials should feel tactile: paper, stone, wood, foliage, woven fabric, ceramic, brushed metal, or calm glass.
- Forms are recognisable but not photorealistic. Detail accumulates softly; avoid hard vector geometry, 3D-plastic rendering, or hyperreal skin.
- Keep edges selectively soft. Reserve sharper detail for the focal object or the immediate foreground.

### Composition

- Build a legible three-depth composition: foreground anchor → middle-ground story → open horizon/quiet field.
- Include a route, sequence, handoff, archive, threshold, or protected place when the message benefits from one. It can be physical or abstract.
- Use generous negative space for UI copy, headlines, or an empty-state message. Do not place dense detail behind text.
- Focal subjects are off-center or modestly centered; they should invite attention, not demand it.
- Prefer one clear story to a collage of symbols. A small meaningful object is stronger than many explanatory icons.

## Subject matter

Use visual metaphors for the value and stewardship of prompt property, not literal legal ownership. The image should connect a human act of care to a durable record, a clear path, or trusted reuse.

| Product idea | Suitable visual direction |
| --- | --- |
| Prompt as personal craft | a worktable, notebook, seedling, carefully kept object, quiet studio shelf |
| Ownership and recognition | a named place, maker’s mark, tended object, carefully assigned shelf—not a deed or certificate |
| Revision history / provenance | a path with gentle turns, layered paper, tree rings, restored archive, evolving textile |
| Trusted revision | a clear marker, well-lit waypoint, finished instrument, signal in a calm landscape |
| Review and evidence | two views in conversation, annotated material, a lens, a careful comparison—not a courtroom |
| Access and privacy | a sheltered room, protected garden, softly lit cabinet, a threshold with visible consent |
| Release and restore | a bridge, handoff point, return path, maintained trail, reversible mechanism |
| Registry and reuse | a calm archive, labelled drawers, constellated index, organised workshop |

Avoid real-estate imagery, deeds, keys as a primary cliché, padlocks as the default security metaphor, blockchain imagery, robots, prompt text floating in space, or generic “AI brain” visuals.

## Context recipes

### Product placeholders and empty states

- Show the value being preserved or made usable: a cared-for object, an open route, an organised collection, or a trustworthy marker.
- Keep the illustration quiet, sparse, and secondary to the action. It should offer orientation, not become a campaign image.
- Use one small narrative scene or object; place it where it does not interrupt scanning or form completion.
- Prefer cropped detail, vignette, or a calm scene with open room around it. Avoid faces, spectacle, and large characters.
- The message and CTA remain the focal point. The illustration is evidence of care, not an instruction.

### Product mockups

- Treat the interface as the primary artifact. Surround it with a lightly painted environment, material, or frame only when it reinforces the story.
- Keep screen content crisp and accurate; do not paint over controls, invent data, or make mock UI illegible.
- Use depth subtly: a paper-like field, desk surface, or atmospheric background is enough. Avoid floating-device stacks and excessive glass effects.

### Slides and editorial covers

- Use a single expansive scene with generous title-safe negative space.
- Let the image introduce the chapter’s emotional idea—preserve, review, trust, release—without duplicating the slide title in visual symbols.
- Cropping may become more cinematic than in product, but retain the same soft light, restrained palette, and considered focal point.

### Social posts and campaign art

- Use a strong single frame, one idea, and readable open space for the message.
- A human trace is welcome: a hand, a desk, a mark of care, or a distant figure. Do not make a mascot the campaign protagonist.
- The image may be more expressive than product placeholder art, but it must remain calm and durable rather than trend-driven.

### Mascot or recurring character

Prope is **not mascot-led**. If a character is ever used, it is a quiet supporting presence rather than a brand personality: small in frame, grounded in a real task, gently stylised, and never delivering jokes, hype, or gamified celebration. A character must not replace ownership, evidence, or clear product language.

## Adjustable art-direction controls

When commissioning or generating an image, specify these controls rather than changing the core style:

| Control | Low | High |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Narrative density | one object / simple atmosphere | a complete but uncluttered scene |
| Abstraction | material and light only | literal, human-readable metaphor |
| Product proximity | editorial world around a concept | UI-ready empty-state support |
| Human presence | no people; traces of care | one understated person or hand |
| Energy | contemplative and still | gently forward-moving, never urgent |

## Prompt framework

Use this as a structured brief for ImageGen or an illustrator:

```text
Create a [context] for Prope that expresses [property value: ownership / preservation / provenance / trusted reuse / protected access].
Product idea: [product idea].
Visual tone: Quiet stewardship — premium painterly digital illustration,
luminous diffused daylight, warm canvas and deep blue-teal atmosphere,
soft olive/earth detail, one restrained yellow-gold focal signal.
Composition: [foreground anchor], [middle-ground story], [open negative space location].
Metaphor: [personal craft becoming durable property / visible owner / preserved history / trusted path / protected place].
Rendering: tactile natural materials, refined soft brush texture,
atmospheric depth, recognisable but not photorealistic forms.
Keep the image calm, precise, and quietly optimistic; make the UI/copy area clear.
Avoid: cyberpunk, neon AI glow, generic robots, padlock cliché, crypto imagery,
hard vector graphics, glossy 3D plastic, crowded composition, mascot-like characters,
text baked into the image, legal ownership symbolism.
```

## Acceptance check

Before using an image, confirm:

- Does it communicate care, continuity, or a trusted next step without explaining itself literally?
- Does it make the prompt feel like valuable work worth preserving, not disposable text or a generic AI output?
- Does it reinforce ownership, history, access, or trusted use without implying legal ownership?
- Is the focal contrast restrained enough that Prope’s UI and yellow action remain primary?
- Is there clear room for the required UI or editorial copy?
- Does it avoid turning “property” into legal or real-estate symbolism?
- Would it still feel calm, premium, and useful after the current visual trend has passed?
