<objective>
Serve the six management routes and give the project its first ASGI application. `Makefile run` has
targeted `prope.app:app` since Phase 1 with a comment saying the module arrives in phase 2; it starts
working when this lands.

Purpose: `PUT /workspaces/{id}/retention` is the write half of the setting plan 02-02 built the read
for, `GET /prices/unpriced` is the route Phase 1's `UNPRICED_WORKLIST` comment says Phase 2 owes, and
`/readyz` ships its final semantics now so Phase 3 never has to delete a readiness rule it inherited.

Output: `schemas.py`, `session_auth.py`, `api.py`, `app.py`, and three test modules — the routes'
behaviour, health and readiness, and the assembled route table.

**Named pre-condition carried from Phase 1: CR-01.** Phase 1's verifier confirmed that
`ck_price_table_prices_are_non_negative` permits a free-tier `(0, 0)` price row, that `compute_cost`
then returns exactly zero, and that `write_records` raises on a zero cost and discards the whole batch.
Its recommended disposition was **(a): accept as a blocking pre-condition on the Phase 2 plan that
builds `POST /api/v1/prices`**, with two named tests — a `(0,0)` row refused at insert, and one
unpriceable record not discarding its batch-mates. As of planning, `01-UAT.md` still reads
`awaiting: user response`, and the absence of an answer is not an answer. **This plan adopts
disposition (a) and builds the first of those two tests; the second lives in plan 02-01's drain failure
classification.** Both halves of CR-01's exposure arrive in this phase, which is exactly why the
verifier routed it here. The developer's confirmation of the disposition is a human check below.

**Stated gap, per D-16.** `/readyz` is the database alone from the first line it is written. A broker
outage is a separate counted-degraded signal, never un-readiness. That is the final semantics and
nothing rewrites it in Phase 3 — but it means that for one phase there is a window where the broker is
down, readiness is green, and records genuinely are lost. CL-01 is what closes it.
</objective>
