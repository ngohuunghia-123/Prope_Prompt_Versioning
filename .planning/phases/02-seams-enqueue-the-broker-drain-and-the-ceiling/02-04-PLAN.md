<objective>
Build the three internal contracts evaluation will be written against, and prove all three from
call-ledger's own tests, because neither caller exists yet.

This plan carries every one of the phase's three success criteria: `check_spend_ceiling` refusing with a
reason and no figure while gateway traffic keeps being recorded; an eval usage row priced, counted and
bodyless; and `get_run_totals` returning the two counts alongside the totals.

Purpose: the two failure contracts are opposite, and that is the whole argument for two functions rather
than one. `enqueue` never raises into the caller, ever, because the gateway's p95 depends on it;
`price_usage` raises, because evaluation must be able to record a run error. A single function with a
`source` flag would have to pick one and quietly break the other caller.

Output: `seams.py` with three functions and three frozen result objects, three integration tests keyed
to the lock file's AC-6, AC-9 and AC-11, and `OQ-019` and `OQ-020` raised against the two upstream files
this plan deviates from.

**No reversibility checkpoint is inserted in this plan, deliberately.** D-18 and D-20 are both rated
one-way, and both were decided by the developer in the phase discussion with that rating recorded. The
door has already been walked through; a checkpoint here would be re-asking a settled question. The
ratings stay on the tasks so the record survives, and the deviations are raised as open questions
against the upstream files rather than absorbed.
</objective>

`CeilingReason` is a `StrEnum` in `domain.py`'s convention, with `MONTHLY_CEILING_EXCEEDED` as its only

choosing a code now is what stops that phase inheriting an untranslatable English sentence.

`CeilingDecision` is a frozen slotted dataclass with exactly two fields, `allowed: bool` and
`reason: CeilingReason | None`. **It has no third field and must never grow one that carries a
number.** Say so at the class, with A-056's reasoning: the ceiling is a boolean surface, there is no
billing dashboard in the MVP, and the refusal is the one place a billing position could leak to somebody

that this be asserted under every branch including refused, which is why the test below asserts the
