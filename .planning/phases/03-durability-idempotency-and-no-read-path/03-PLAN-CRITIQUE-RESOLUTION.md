## One thing a later stage should carry forward

Findings SPEC-6, SPEC-7 and SPEC-9-clause-5 all had the same shape: a lock-file clause that ROADMAP's
success criteria happen not to restate, in the last phase of its milestone. **`REQUIREMENTS.md` maps a
requirement to an acceptance criterion, not to a success criterion**, so a plan set that satisfies
every ROADMAP SC can still leave an AC clause unasserted — and once the milestone closes, nobody is
looking. All three are closed here rather than routed onward, but the pattern is worth checking at
every later milestone-closing phase (9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24) as a matter of course.
