<objective>
Close CL-10 by **enumerating**, not by asserting.

AC-10's words are *"the module's entire HTTP **and internal** surface"*, and Phase 2 asserted the
first third of that: `tests/architecture/test_route_table.py` walks the assembled table and the
OpenAPI schema for `FORBIDDEN_FIELD_NAMES`, and its own docstring says *"CL-10's full claim is Phase
3's."* The two layers it does not reach are the two that are actually at risk:

- **The internal Python surface.** `get_run_totals` is a function, not a route. A body reaching a
  caller through a returned dataclass would satisfy **every route-table assertion in this
  repository**, and would keep satisfying them for twenty-one more phases.
- **The statements.** `prope_app`'s privileges on `ledger.call_body` are `SELECT, INSERT, DELETE`, and
  the `SELECT` is load-bearing — the retention `DELETE ... WHERE expires_at <= $1` reads a column, and
  after plan 03-02 the body upsert's `ON CONFLICT` reads one too. **So the database cannot be the
  guarantee**, and `0003_ledger_tables.py:246-252` says so in a comment addressed to this phase by
  name: *"AC-10's no-read-path guarantee is a property of the ROUTE TABLE (Phase 3), not of the grant
  matrix."* The thing that stands in for a grant is an enumeration of every statement in `src/` that
  names the table, asserted as a set.

And a fourth reader that none of the three layers can see. `prope_report` is created
`LOGIN BYPASSRLS` (`alembic/versions/0001_roles_and_schemas.py:47`), and `grant_table`'s
`report_select` default gave it `SELECT` on `ledger.call_body` in the revision that created the table.
**One statement on that credential reads every stored body in every workspace, RLS bypassed by role
attribute.** `tests/conftest.py:162-167` even builds that engine as a session fixture. AC-10's text —
*"route, parameter, or function"* — does not reach a database role; `security_requirements`' first
clause — *"no endpoint, **no export**, no admin viewer, and no join from another module's query"* —
arguably does. Task 3 is that decision, written out and adopted, and **the one thing this plan refuses
outright is shipping the sweep silently incomplete**: a year later, "we looked and decided not to" and
"nobody looked" are indistinguishable, which is the failure this repository's whole verification
posture is shaped against.

**What this plan is not.** It is not the cross-module form of AC-10 — that needs eight modules mounted
and is **Phase 23**'s (PITFALLS §5). This plan writes the single-module form using the shared helpers
(`prope.session_auth.assembled_routes`, `declared_action`) so Phase 23 extends a test rather than
inventing one. And it is not a narrowing of `prope_app`'s grants: that grant is deliberately not
narrow, and narrowing it would break the retention job.
</objective>
